# Weekly Review SOP

## Purpose

Use this rhythm once per week to convert BOSSA signals into decisions and actions.

## Cadence

Recommended time: Monday morning or before the first weekly planning block.

## Inputs Needed

- Last week revenue
- Last week covers
- Target revenue
- Target covers
- Competitor notes
- Promo pressure
- Open decisions
- Active actions
- Guest or staff feedback themes

## 20-Minute Review Flow

### 1. Scan the Dashboard — 5 minutes

Check:

- Top Threat
- Promo Pressure
- Open Decisions
- High-priority actions
- Weekly Brief

### 2. Validate Signals — 5 minutes

For every high-priority signal, decide:

| Decision | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Keep | Signal is valid and actionable |
| Watch | Signal matters but needs more data |
| Ignore | Signal is noise |

### 3. Close or Delegate Decisions — 5 minutes

Every open decision must become one of these:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Done | Decision made |
| Assigned | Owner has next action |
| Parked | Not relevant this week |

### 4. Lock the Action List — 5 minutes

Each action needs:

- Owner
- Priority
- Due date
- Status
- Success signal

## Weekly Output

At the end of the review, BOSSA should have:

1. One clear weekly move
2. Maximum three priority actions
3. One metric to watch
4. One lesson to log

## Rule

Do not add more actions if open decisions are not closed.
